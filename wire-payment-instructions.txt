SECTION 1: INTRODUCTION

Welcome to our comprehensive Wire Payment Instructions guide. This document
provides detailed information on how to initiate, process, and receive wire
transfers for both domestic and international transactions.

1.1 PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT
This guide serves as the official reference for all wire payment procedures
within our organization. It is designed to ensure:
   • Accuracy in payment processing
   • Compliance with banking regulations
   • Security of financial transactions
   • Efficiency in fund transfers
   • Proper documentation and audit trails

1.2 SCOPE
This document covers:
   • Domestic wire transfers (within the United States)
   • International wire transfers (cross-border payments)
   • Same-day and next-day processing options
   • Security protocols and authentication procedures
   • Troubleshooting common issues
   • Regulatory compliance requirements

1.3 IMPORTANT NOTICES

⚠ SECURITY ALERT: Always verify wire payment instructions through multiple
channels before processing. Be aware of Business Email Compromise (BEC) scams.

⚠ TIMING: Wire transfers are typically irreversible once processed. Ensure
all information is accurate before submission.

⚠ FEES: Wire transfer fees apply to both domestic and international
transactions. Please refer to the current fee schedule.

1.4 KEY DEFINITIONS

Wire Transfer: Electronic transfer of funds from one bank or credit union
to another, typically processed through SWIFT or Fedwire networks.

SWIFT Code: Society for Worldwide Interbank Financial Telecommunication code,
used for international wire transfers (also known as BIC - Bank Identifier Code).

ABA Routing Number: Nine-digit code used to identify U.S. financial
institutions for domestic wire transfers.

IBAN: International Bank Account Number, used in many countries for
international transfers.

Beneficiary: The recipient of the wire transfer funds.

Originator: The person or entity initiating the wire transfer.

SECTION 2: DOMESTIC WIRE TRANSFERS

2.1 REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR DOMESTIC WIRES

To process a domestic wire transfer within the United States, you must
provide the following information:

BENEFICIARY INFORMATION:
   • Full Legal Name (as it appears on the bank account)
   • Complete Bank Account Number
   • Bank's ABA Routing Number (9 digits)
   • Bank Name
   • Bank Address (Street, City, State, ZIP Code)
   • Account Type (Checking or Savings)

ORIGINATOR INFORMATION:
   • Your Full Name or Company Name
   • Your Account Number
   • Your Contact Phone Number
   • Purpose of Payment (brief description)
   • Payment Amount (in USD)

2.2 STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS FOR INITIATING DOMESTIC WIRES

STEP 1: VERIFICATION
   a) Log into your secure banking portal
   b) Navigate to "Wire Transfers" section
   c) Select "Domestic Wire Transfer"
   d) Verify your account has sufficient funds plus fees

STEP 2: ENTER BENEFICIARY DETAILS
   a) Input beneficiary bank's ABA routing number
   b) System will auto-populate bank name (verify accuracy)
   c) Enter beneficiary account number
   d) Enter beneficiary name exactly as shown on account
   e) Add bank address information

STEP 3: ENTER PAYMENT DETAILS
   a) Enter transfer amount in USD
   b) Provide payment purpose/description
   c) Select processing speed (same-day or next-day)
   d) Review current wire transfer fees

STEP 4: REVIEW AND AUTHORIZE
   a) Carefully review all information entered
   b) Verify amount and beneficiary details
   c) Authorize with secure authentication method
   d) Save confirmation number for records

SECTION 3: INTERNATIONAL WIRE TRANSFERS

3.1 REQUIRED INFORMATION FOR INTERNATIONAL WIRES

International wire transfers require additional information compared to
domestic transfers:

BENEFICIARY BANK INFORMATION:
   • Full Bank Name
   • Bank's SWIFT/BIC Code (8 or 11 characters)
   • Bank's Full Address (Street, City, Country)
   • IBAN (if applicable for European/Middle Eastern banks)
   • Bank Branch Name (if applicable)

INTERMEDIARY BANK (if required):
   • Intermediary Bank Name
   • Intermediary Bank SWIFT Code
   • Intermediary Bank Address

BENEFICIARY INFORMATION:
   • Full Legal Name (exactly as appears on account)
   • Complete Account Number or IBAN
   • Full Address (Street, City, Postal Code, Country)
   • Phone Number (with country code)
   • Purpose of Payment (detailed description)

ADDITIONAL REQUIRED INFORMATION:
   • Currency of payment
   • Payment amount
   • Fee payment method (OUR/BEN/SHA)
   • Regulatory information (if required by country)

3.2 UNDERSTANDING SWIFT CODES

SWIFT Code Format: AAAABBCCDDD

Where:
   AAAA = Bank Code (4 letters)
   BB = Country Code (2 letters)
   CC = Location Code (2 letters or digits)
   DDD = Branch Code (3 letters or digits, optional)

Example: CHASUS33XXX
   CHAS = JPMorgan Chase Bank
   US = United States
   33 = New York
   XXX = Head Office

3.3 UNDERSTANDING FEE PAYMENT OPTIONS

When sending international wires, you must specify who pays the fees:

OUR (All charges paid by sender):
   • Sender pays all fees (originating, intermediary, and receiving)
   • Beneficiary receives full payment amount
   • Most expensive option but ensures full payment to recipient
   • Recommended for: Critical payments, contractual obligations

BEN (All charges paid by beneficiary):
   • Sender pays only outgoing wire fee
   • All other fees deducted from payment amount
   • Beneficiary receives amount minus intermediary/receiving fees
   • Least expensive for sender
   • Recommended for: Internal transfers, when pre-agreed with beneficiary

SHA (Shared charges - Default option):
   • Sender pays outgoing wire fee
   • Beneficiary pays incoming wire fee
   • Intermediary fees (if any) deducted from payment amount
   • Most common option for international transfers
   • Recommended for: Standard business transactions

3.4 STEP-BY-STEP PROCESS FOR INTERNATIONAL WIRES

STEP 1: GATHER ALL REQUIRED INFORMATION
   • Collect complete beneficiary and bank details
   • Verify SWIFT code using online SWIFT directory
   • Confirm IBAN format for European transfers
   • Determine if intermediary bank is needed

STEP 2: CURRENCY AND AMOUNT
   • Select payment currency (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, etc.)
   • Enter payment amount
   • Review current exchange rate (if applicable)
   • Calculate total cost including fees

STEP 3: COMPLETE WIRE TRANSFER FORM
   • Access international wire transfer section
   • Enter all beneficiary information
   • Input beneficiary bank details
   • Add intermediary bank (if required)
   • Specify fee payment method (OUR/BEN/SHA)

STEP 4: PURPOSE OF PAYMENT
   • Provide detailed payment purpose
   • Include invoice numbers or reference numbers
   • Ensure compliance with destination country regulations
   • Some countries require specific purpose codes

STEP 5: REVIEW AND SUBMIT
   • Verify all information carefully
   • Check SWIFT code and account number
   • Confirm currency and amount
   • Authorize transaction with multi-factor authentication
   • Save confirmation number and receipt

SECTION 4: SECURITY PROTOCOLS AND COMPLIANCE

4.1 AUTHENTICATION REQUIREMENTS

All wire transfers require multi-factor authentication:

TIER 1 - Up to $10,000:
   • Username and password
   • One-time SMS verification code
   • Security question

TIER 2 - $10,001 to $100,000:
   • Username and password
   • Mobile app authentication token
   • Email confirmation
   • Phone verification callback

TIER 3 - Over $100,000:
   • Username and password
   • Hardware security token
   • Email confirmation
   • Phone verification callback
   • Dual authorization (two authorized signers)

4.2 FRAUD PREVENTION BEST PRACTICES

VERIFY PAYMENT INSTRUCTIONS:
   ✓ Always verify wire instructions through a known phone number
   ✓ Call the beneficiary directly using a number from your records
   ✓ Never rely solely on email for wire instruction changes
   ✓ Be suspicious of urgent or unusual payment requests

RECOGNIZE COMMON SCAM INDICATORS:
   ⚠ Requests to change payment instructions at the last minute
   ⚠ Pressure to process payment urgently
   ⚠ Email from a similar but not exact email address
   ⚠ Requests for payment to unfamiliar countries or banks
   ⚠ Instructions that differ from standard procedures

SECURE YOUR CREDENTIALS:
   ✓ Never share your banking password or authentication codes
   ✓ Use strong, unique passwords for banking access
   ✓ Enable all available security features
   ✓ Log out of banking sessions when complete
   ✓ Monitor your accounts regularly for unauthorized activity

4.3 BUSINESS EMAIL COMPROMISE (BEC) AWARENESS

Business Email Compromise is a sophisticated scam where criminals:
   • Compromise legitimate business email accounts
   • Impersonate executives or vendors
   • Request fraudulent wire transfers
   • Use urgent language to bypass normal procedures

PROTECTION STRATEGIES:
   1. Implement dual authorization for all wire transfers
   2. Establish verbal verification procedures
   3. Train employees on BEC recognition
   4. Use encrypted communication for sensitive information
   5. Maintain an approved vendor/beneficiary list
   6. Question any changes to established payment procedures

IF YOU SUSPECT FRAUD:
   ➤ STOP - Do not process the wire transfer
   ➤ CALL - Contact your bank's fraud department immediately
   ➤ VERIFY - Independently verify instructions through known contacts
   ➤ REPORT - File a report with appropriate authorities

Emergency Fraud Hotline: 1-800-555-FRAU (3728)
Available 24/7/365

4.4 REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

OFFICE OF FOREIGN ASSETS CONTROL (OFAC):
All wire transfers are screened against OFAC sanctions lists. Transfers
involving the following are prohibited:
   • Sanctioned countries (e.g., North Korea, Iran, Syria)
   • Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) list individuals/entities
   • Blocked persons or entities

BANK SECRECY ACT (BSA) / ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML):
Wire transfers are subject to BSA/AML requirements:
   • Large cash transaction reporting (over $10,000)
   • Suspicious activity monitoring and reporting
   • Customer due diligence procedures
   • Enhanced due diligence for high-risk transactions

KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC):
Banks must verify:
   • Identity of account holders
   • Source of funds
   • Purpose of transactions
   • Beneficial ownership of entities

USA PATRIOT ACT:
Requirements include:
   • Customer identification program compliance
   • Beneficial ownership verification
   • Information sharing among financial institutions
   • Enhanced scrutiny of correspondent accounts

SECTION 5: TROUBLESHOOTING AND SUPPORT

5.1 COMMON ISSUES AND SOLUTIONS

ISSUE: Wire transfer is delayed
POSSIBLE CAUSES:
   • Incorrect beneficiary information
   • Missing or incomplete required fields
   • Compliance review in progress
   • Intermediary bank delays
   • Beneficiary bank holiday or weekend
   • Currency conversion processing

SOLUTIONS:
   ✓ Check wire status in online banking portal
   ✓ Verify all information is correct
   ✓ Contact wire transfer department for status update
   ✓ Request trace if delayed more than 5 business days

ISSUE: Wire transfer returned
POSSIBLE CAUSES:
   • Incorrect account number
   • Invalid SWIFT code or routing number
   • Account closed or frozen
   • Beneficiary name mismatch
   • Regulatory rejection

SOLUTIONS:
   ✓ Review returned wire notification
   ✓ Verify beneficiary account details
   ✓ Contact beneficiary to confirm information
   ✓ Resubmit with corrected information
   ✓ Expect refund within 5-10 business days

ISSUE: Beneficiary didn't receive full amount
POSSIBLE CAUSES:
   • Intermediary bank fees deducted
   • Receiving bank fees applied
   • Currency conversion charges
   • Fee option was BEN or SHA instead of OUR

SOLUTIONS:
   ✓ Review fee payment option selected
   ✓ Request detailed fee breakdown
   ✓ Send additional payment to cover fees
   ✓ Use OUR option for future critical payments

ISSUE: Need to cancel wire transfer
PROCESS:
   1. Contact wire department immediately
   2. Provide wire confirmation number
   3. Explain reason for cancellation
   4. Cancellation only possible if not yet processed
   5. Cancellation fee applies ($30-$65)
   6. Refund processed within 3-5 business days

ISSUE: Need to amend wire transfer
PROCESS:
   1. Contact wire department immediately
   2. Provide wire confirmation number and account details
   3. Specify information to be amended
   4. Amendment only possible if not yet received by beneficiary bank
   5. Amendment fee applies ($20-$50)
   6. May require wire recall and resubmission

5.2 WIRE TRACING PROCEDURES

When to Request a Wire Trace:
   • Wire not received within expected timeframe
   • Confirmation of delivery needed
   • Dispute resolution
   • Proof of payment required

How to Request a Trace:
   1. Gather wire confirmation number
   2. Contact wire transfer department
   3. Complete trace request form
   4. Pay trace fee ($25)
   5. Allow 5-10 business days for results

Trace Results Include:
   • Path of wire through banking system
   • Date and time of delivery
   • Receiving bank confirmation
   • Account credited information
   • Any fees deducted along the way

5.7 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)

Q: How long does a domestic wire take?
A: Domestic wires are typically completed within 2-4 hours if submitted
before cutoff time, or next business day if after cutoff.

Q: Can I cancel a wire transfer?
A: Yes, but only if it hasn't been processed yet. Contact us immediately
with your confirmation number. Cancellation fees apply.

Q: What's the difference between ACH and wire transfer?
A: Wire transfers are faster (same day to 1-2 days) but more expensive.
ACH transfers take 1-3 business days but have lower fees.

Q: Do I need SWIFT code for domestic wires?
A: No, domestic wires within the US only require ABA routing number.

Q: What if I made a mistake in beneficiary information?
A: Contact us immediately. If not yet processed, we can amend. If already
sent, we'll need to request a recall, which may not be successful.

Q: Are wire transfers safe?
A: Yes, when proper procedures are followed. Always verify instructions
through multiple channels and be aware of fraud attempts.

Q: How much can I wire transfer?
A: Limits vary by account type and authorization level. Contact your
relationship manager for your specific limits.

5.8 GLOSSARY OF TERMS

ABA Routing Number: Nine-digit code identifying US financial institutions
Beneficiary: The recipient of a wire transfer
IBAN: International Bank Account Number
Intermediary Bank: Bank that facilitates transfer between sender and receiver
SWIFT: Network for secure financial messaging between banks
Originator: Person or entity sending the wire transfer
OUR/BEN/SHA: Fee payment options for international wires
Fedwire: Real-time gross settlement system operated by Federal Reserve
CHIPS: Clearing House Interbank Payments System
Settlement: Final completion of a wire transfer
Trace: Investigation to track a wire transfer through the banking system
